## Direction contract

THESIS: A run leaves objects behind, so catalogue them. Each view is a catalogue spread: on the left page, a plate, one real artefact the pipeline made (the field inventory, the request, the extract, the browser's page-load log, the declared manifest, the HL7 message as it left, the retention record, the five runs, the audit chain); on the right page, its catalogue entry with one fact set monumental. Comparanda a / b / c (ours, an AI model, the baseline) are flipped in place on the same plate. It refuses the dashboard, the pipeline diagram and the side-by-side race.

OWN-WORLD: A printed museum catalogue: white page, near-black ink, graded greys for what was not taken, and one red that appears only for a breach. **Colour pass, 2026-10-15 (user: "make the v3 bit more catchy and feel and color"):** each comparandum prints on its own stock -- ours viridian (#0b7a62 on a #dbeee6 plate), the AI model ultramarine (#3b45b0 on #e3e5f8), the baseline ochre (#a3610f on #f5e7cd); shared plates (hospital, request, index) stay on stone grey; flipping a/b/c re-tints the plate and the monumental figure; the comparanda letters are stock-coloured tabs; a cover (frontispiece) opens the catalogue: the three stocks as a swatch fan on a night plate, each blade carrying the values that technique took for the job, beside the title set wide and a contents list. One face, Archivo, its width axis doing the work: figures set wide and light (wdth 125, weight 200-250) at monumental size, entries at normal width, logs and lists semi-condensed; platform mono only for HL7, the retention record and the audit lines. Hairline rules, no cards, no pills; shadows only where paper lies on paper or on the table (the book, the tipped-in plate, a turning leaf, the cover's blades). Raised by the declined challengers, each a named discipline: **Type is the material** (alphabet storm): the figure is the image and a baseline's 110-line log fills its plate as type; **Dated provenance, known and attested** (provenance ribbon): demonstrated controls carry a solid rule, attested ones a dashed hairline, unsubstantiated ones a red strike; the custody plate is a dated, hash-linked chain; **Colour is earned** (risograph, emergency broadcast): colour names a comparandum's stock or a breach, nothing else; **Plates keyed by number** (teletext): digits jump straight to a plate; **One geometry** (zoo map): every view is the same spread, and plates 1 and 3 are the same inventory.

STORY: The hospital holds 680 values. The job asks for 7 fields of one patient. Ours takes 7, the model a few more, the baseline all 680 and 19 other patients. It cost 5 page loads, or 110. Ours declared what it relied on and the register backs it; the baseline declared almost nothing. Ours left as a token and is erased on its date; the baseline left raw and is kept indefinitely. A model may answer differently on its next run; ours cannot. The audit chain shows whose record was read and when it went.
